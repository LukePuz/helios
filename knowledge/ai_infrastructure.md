# AI Infrastructure Thesis

## Conviction: Very High
## Duration: Now + 10 years
## Timing: Active — already in acceleration phase

---

## Core View

AI infrastructure represents the largest capital investment cycle in technology history.
This is a decade-long structural transformation, not a cyclical trend.

Value creation will shift across the ecosystem as different bottlenecks emerge and resolve.
The investment framework is not "buy AI exposure" — it is:

1. Identify where the current bottleneck is forming
2. Identify who owns the scarce resource
3. Confirm pricing power exists
4. Assess whether expectations are already priced in

---

## Bottleneck Framework

### Current and Emerging Bottlenecks (priority order)

| Priority | Bottleneck | Status | Primary Beneficiaries |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Memory (HBM, DRAM) | Actively constraining | MU, SNDK, KLAC, LRCX, AMAT |
| 2 | Power availability | Emerging — 2027+ critical | VRT, GEV, CEG, PWR |
| 3 | Data center capacity | Active | CLS, CRWV, EQIX |
| 4 | Networking / connectivity | Active | AVGO, ANET, CRDO, MRVL |
| 5 | Capital / hyperscaler spend cycles | Ongoing | NVDA, AMD, MSFT, AMZN, GOOG |

### Bottleneck Analysis Template

For each bottleneck:
- What is the evidence of constraint? (pricing, lead times, utilization)
- Is the constraint worsening or resolving?
- Who has pricing power because of it?
- Is growth accelerating for the beneficiary?
- Is the valuation already assuming the constraint persists?
- What is the catalyst to either worsen or resolve?

---

## Primary Drivers

- AI compute demand growing faster than supply across all dimensions
- GPU, CPU, and custom silicon demand
- High-bandwidth memory becoming critical bottleneck
- Data center networking upgrades (400G → 800G → 1.6T)
- Power delivery as data center density increases
- Hyperscaler capex commitments continuing at elevated levels

## Investment Approach

### Valuation Philosophy

Premium valuations are acceptable when supported by:
- Superior growth rate (30%+ revenue growth)
- Strategic positioning at a bottleneck
- Durable competitive advantage
- Expanding total addressable market

Compare opportunities across the ecosystem — not just within sub-segments.
A less expensive, slower-growing enabler may offer better risk/reward than
a high-multiple leader where expectations are already stretched.

### Preferred Vehicles

| Vehicle | Use Case |
|---|---|
| Core equity positions | Long-duration compounders (NVDA, AVGO, ANET) |
| LEAPS | Capital-efficient exposure to highest conviction names |
| Tactical options (45–90 DTE) | Pre-catalyst, pre-earnings asymmetric setups |
| Momentum trades | Breakout setups on bottleneck beneficiaries |

---

## Portfolio Allocation

AI infrastructure may represent 50%+ of portfolio when conditions support:
- Active bottleneck with clear beneficiaries
- Multiple layers of the value chain offer opportunities simultaneously
- Regime is Risk-On or better

Reduce exposure when:
1. AI capex slows meaningfully (watch hyperscaler guidance)
2. Valuation becomes disconnected from any reasonable growth path
3. Macro conditions materially deteriorate (regime drops to Risk-Off)

Do NOT reduce solely due to:
- Normal volatility
- Short-term corrections
- Sector rotation
- Market fear (VIX spikes that are not thesis-breaking)

## Neo Clouds / AI Compute Specialists

### Conviction: Moderate
### Timing: 2026–2028

Core thesis: Specialized AI compute providers may capture value during infrastructure scaling
before hyperscalers fully commoditize GPU access.

### Primary Upside Drivers

- GPU scarcity persists longer than expected
- Demand for burst compute exceeds hyperscaler capacity
- Contracted enterprise demand allows pricing power

### Red Flags (exit signals)

- Debt growing faster than revenue
- GPU capacity becomes commoditized rapidly
- Pricing compression from hyperscalers
- Low utilization rates
- No path to cash flow positive
