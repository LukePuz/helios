# AI Software / Agent Economy Thesis

## Conviction: Very High
## Timing: 2026–2028 acceleration
## Duration: 5–10 year structural shift

---

## Core View

AI agents are becoming a major computing paradigm shift.
Software companies that embed AI agents into enterprise workflows
will see both expansion of existing revenue and creation of entirely new categories.

Value capture will not be uniform. Winners will be determined by:
- Data advantage (proprietary data moats)
- Workflow depth (hard-to-replace integrations)
- Platform reach (distribution and ecosystem)
- AI-native architecture (built for agents, not bolted on)

---

## Value Capture Framework

| Layer | Who Wins | Why |
|---|---|---|
| AI Platform / Infrastructure | Hyperscalers (MSFT, GOOG, AMZN, META) | Distribution, compute, model access |
| AI-Enabled Enterprise Software | PLTR, NOW, CRM, SNOW, ORCL | Workflow depth, data, switching costs |
| AI-Native Categories | New entrants — watch space | No legacy to disrupt |
| Vertical AI | Sector-specific winners | Domain expertise + data |
| Consumer AI Agents | Hyperscalers + new platforms | Distribution |

---

## Primary Drivers

- Enterprise AI adoption moving from experimentation to production workflows
- SaaS disruption: AI agents automate tasks previously requiring human SaaS users → threatens per-seat models but rewards outcome-based pricing leaders
- Hyperscaler AI platforms providing model access, reducing barrier to AI deployment
- Vertical AI applications creating new market categories
- Consumer AI agents creating new distribution and monetization models

---

## Key Investment Question

For every software company: Is AI expanding their TAM or threatening their model?

- **Expanding:** AI creates new workflows, new revenue streams, higher retention
- **Threatening:** AI commoditizes the core product or replaces the human who uses it

The most dangerous position: a SaaS company whose product is easily replicated by a general AI agent.
The best position: a company whose data or workflow integration makes them the AI agent's operating layer.

## Thesis Risks

| Risk | Description |
|---|---|
| Open source compression | Open source AI models commoditize proprietary AI software value |
| Slower enterprise adoption | Enterprises move slower than expected — delays revenue impact |

| Hyperscaler dominance | MSFT, GOOG, AMZN capture all enterprise AI value through bundling |
| New entrant disruption | AI-native startups displace incumbents before they adapt |

## Analyst Evaluation Template

For each AI software company, assess:

1. Does AI expand their TAM or cannibalize their model?
2. What is the proprietary data advantage?
3. How deeply embedded are their workflows? (switching cost)
4. Is the company priced for AI optionality (cheap) or AI success (expensive)?
5. What is the near-term catalyst to prove out AI revenue?
6. Who is the most dangerous competitor — a hyperscaler or an AI-native startup?
7. What does revenue growth look like post-AI vs. pre-AI?
