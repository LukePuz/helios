# Portfolio Philosophy

## Primary Mission

Maximize long-term capital compounding through disciplined,
concentrated, asymmetric investing in the most important
technology transitions of the current era.

This is not a trading system. It is a long-duration compounding system
that uses active management to maximize participation in structural winners
while protecting against thesis failures and macro deterioration.

---

## Core Beliefs

1. The best returns come from owning exceptional businesses during major technology transitions — and holding them long enough for the thesis to compound.

2. Concentration in high-conviction ideas outperforms diversification over long periods. Diversification manages ignorance; conviction manages opportunity.

3. The role of active management is to add to winners, exit failures, and exploit market dislocations — not to trade for activity's sake.

4. Risk management exists to prevent catastrophic losses, not to prevent drawdowns. Drawdowns on intact theses are opportunities, not failures.

5. Cash is a weapon, not a failure. Strategic cash enables deployment into dislocations.

## Portfolio Buckets

### Compounders (Core)

Role: Long-duration wealth creation engine.

Characteristics:
- Category leaders with durable competitive advantages
- Revenue visibility (recurring, contracted, or structural)
- Long-term structural tailwind (10+ years)
- Ability to reinvest capital at high returns

Target allocation: 40–70% of equity

Behavior:
- Largest sizing allowed
- Widest stop tolerance
- Hold through volatility if thesis intact
- Add on conviction increases, not just price weakness

Examples: NVDA, MSFT, AMZN, GOOG, PLTR, AVGO

### Accelerators (Satellite)

Role: Growth expansion through adoption curve participation.

Characteristics:
- Revenue growing rapidly (>30% YoY or at inflection)
- Category leadership emerging but not fully established
- Higher valuation sensitivity — both to the upside and downside
- Catalyst-driven — needs continued validation to hold full size

Target allocation: 20–40% of equity

Behavior:
- Moderate sizing
- Tighter stops than Compounders
- Trim if catalyst passes without thesis confirmation
- Add aggressively if thesis accelerates

Examples: VRT, CRDO, MU, ANET, CEG, CLS, CRWV

### Moonshots (Speculative)

Role: Asymmetric upside with strictly controlled exposure.

Characteristics:
- High potential, high uncertainty
- Long timeline to commercial viability
- Binary or near-binary risk profile
- Small position by design — the sizing IS the risk management

Target allocation: 5–10% of equity (hard cap)

Behavior:
- Strict size caps — never exceed 2% per name
- Options preferred when available (structurally limits downside)
- Never average down
- Rapid reassessment after negative catalyst

Examples: IONQ, RGTI, OKLO, RKLB, QUBT

### Concentration Philosophy

Default posture is highly concentrated.

Increase concentration when:
- Thesis is strengthening and being confirmed by data
- Market regime is Risk-On or better
- Category leadership is emerging clearly
- Risk/reward is improving

Increase diversification when:
- Macro environment is uncertain (Neutral regime)
- Thesis confidence has declined across multiple positions
- Valuations have become stretched across a theme

**Rule:** 5 great ideas at meaningful size outperform 20 mediocre ideas at trivial size.

## Capital Allocation Philosophy

### Deployment Principles

- Start with conviction — meaningful initial position, not a token
- Scale with validation — add as the thesis is confirmed by data
- Use technicals for timing — entry and add timing, not thesis decisions
- Never chase — do not add to a position solely because it has risen significantly

### Cash Management

Cash is strategic, not a default.

| Condition | Cash Target |
|---|---|
| Strong Risk-On regime | 5–10% |
| Risk-On regime | 10–15% |
| Neutral regime | 15–25% |
| Risk-Off regime | 25–40% |
| Strong Risk-Off | 40%+ |

Deploy cash when:
- Regime improves
- High-conviction setup with near-term catalyst
- VIX spike creates asymmetric entry
- Existing name pulls back to strong support while thesis intact

Raise cash when:
- Regime deteriorates
- Multiple theses showing stress simultaneously
- Valuations disconnected from any reasonable growth path
- No high-conviction deployable opportunities

### Compounding Mentality

- Let winners run when thesis is intact
- Avoid forced portfolio rebalancing — selling winners to buy losers is usually wrong
- A position growing to 15% of portfolio through gains is a sign of success, not a problem
- Trim when: valuation is stretched, risk/reward inverted, or capital is better deployed elsewhere

## Options Strategy

Options are a tool for asymmetry — not a substitute for equity conviction.

### Core Equity Positions

All long-duration Compounder positions are held as shares.
Options are not used as the primary vehicle for Compounder exposure.

### Options Are Used For

- Pre-catalyst asymmetric setups (30–90 DTE)
- Limiting downside on higher-risk Accelerator or Moonshot positions
- LEAPS on highest-conviction Compounders as a capital-efficient alternative (90–180 DTE)
- Protective puts on large positions when regime deteriorates

### Options Discipline

- Preferred DTE: 45–90 for tactical; 90–180 for LEAPS
- Never buy options < 21 DTE on a new position
- Always use limit orders for options — never market orders
- Define exit at entry: profit target and max loss (50% premium)
- Total options premium at risk: ≤5% of portfolio

## Drawdown Philosophy

### Risk Tolerance

Moderate drawdown tolerance. The system accepts interim losses in exchange for
long-duration compounding. It does not seek to eliminate volatility —
it seeks to eliminate catastrophic, thesis-breaking losses.

### Protection Mechanisms

| Mechanism | When Used |
|---|---|
| Position sizing | Always — first line of defense |
| Stop losses | Defined at entry for all positions |
| Trailing stops | After meaningful gains on intact thesis |
| Regime-based reduction | When macro environment deteriorates |
| Thesis monitoring | Ongoing — exit if fundamental thesis breaks |

### When to Hold Through Drawdown

- Thesis is intact (fundamentals, competitive position, industry dynamics unchanged)
- Market regime supports holding
- Position is sized appropriately (not oversized)
- Stop level has not been triggered

### When to Exit Despite Thesis

- Stop level triggered
- Management credibility broken (guidance miss + lowered outlook)
- Competitive position has materially weakened
- Capital can be more productively deployed elsewhere with better risk/reward

## Watchlist Philosophy

The watchlist is a maintained universe of tracked ideas.

### Composition

Human-curated (core):
- High-conviction thesis tickers
- Long-term structural themes
- Strategic ideas under development

AI-discovered (supplemental):
- New supply chain beneficiaries
- Emerging category leaders
- Catalyst opportunities outside core coverage

### Conviction Tiers

| Tier | Description | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Highest conviction, ready to act | Full Analyst + Risk evaluation |
| Watch | Interesting but needs validation | Scanner monitoring |
| Speculative | High upside, uncertain timing | Moonshot sizing when acted upon |

## What This System Does Not Optimize For

- Maximum trade frequency
- Short-term win rate
- Beating a benchmark index
- Constant activity and rebalancing
- Minimizing all drawdowns at the expense of upside

**The metric that matters: Long-term CAGR.**
Everything else is subordinate to this objective.
