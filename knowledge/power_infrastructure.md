# Power Infrastructure Thesis

## Conviction: Very High
## Timing: 2027–2030 critical phase
## Duration: 10+ year infrastructure buildout

---

## Core View

Power availability will become the primary constraint on AI scaling.
Every AI data center requires massive, reliable electricity.
The grid is not built for this demand. The companies that solve
electricity generation, delivery, and data center power management
will capture multi-year, high-margin infrastructure revenue.

This thesis has two distinct timelines:
- **Near-term (2025–2027):** Data center electrical systems, UPS, cooling — active now
- **Medium-term (2027–2030):** Grid capacity, nuclear baseload, bring-your-own-power solutions

---

## Why Power Becomes the Bottleneck

1. AI training clusters require 10–100+ MW per facility
2. Grid interconnection queues are years long in most US markets
3. Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) availability is tightening
4. Data center density is increasing (more watts per rack), requiring new power delivery architecture
5. New nuclear is the only scalable 24/7 carbon-free solution at data center scale

### 2. Nuclear / Baseload Power (2027–2030 critical)

Nuclear is the only scalable, 24/7, carbon-free power solution that can supply
the consistent electricity AI data centers need at gigawatt scale.

Near-term: Existing nuclear plants extending licenses and selling power via PPAs to hyperscalers.
Medium-term: New small modular reactors (SMRs) becoming first commercial deployments.
Long-term: SMRs deployed on or near data center campuses.

Invest in Phase 1 now. Begin positioning in Phase 2 names as catalysts emerge.
Phase 3 is Moonshot territory — small sizing, catalyst-driven entry only.

---

## Power Opportunity Score

For each power company, evaluate:

1. Does it directly enable data center scaling today?
2. Does it reduce grid dependency (BYOP or nuclear)?
3. Does it shorten deployment timelines vs. alternatives?
4. Does it have pricing power (contracted backlog, limited competition)?
5. Does AI demand materially change its growth trajectory vs. prior baseline?
6. Is management guiding to accelerating data center revenue?
