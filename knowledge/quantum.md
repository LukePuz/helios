# Quantum Computing Thesis

## Conviction: High
## Timing: 2026–2030 (early milestones); commercial scale 2030+
## Portfolio Bucket: Moonshot (most names)

---

## Core View

Quantum computing has the potential to become a transformative computing paradigm
for a narrow but extremely valuable set of problems.

This is not a short-term investment. It is a multi-year option on a potential
paradigm shift — sized accordingly.

The investment approach: track technical milestones and commercial adoption signals.
Size as Moonshot until commercial revenue is real and growing.
Graduate to Accelerator when sustained enterprise revenue is demonstrated.

---

## Why Quantum Matters

Classical computers solve most problems efficiently.
Quantum computers offer potential exponential advantage for:

1. **Optimization problems:** Supply chain, financial portfolio, logistics routing
2. **Molecular simulation:** Drug discovery, materials science, battery chemistry
3. **Cryptography:** Breaking RSA encryption (long-term threat) + post-quantum cryptography solutions
4. **Machine learning acceleration:** Quantum-enhanced training (theoretical, not yet proven at scale)
5. **Financial modeling:** Complex risk models, derivative pricing

The key word is *potential*. Most of these use cases are not commercially available today.

---

## Investment Drivers

### A. New Compute Layer

Quantum becomes a complementary compute layer alongside CPUs, GPUs, and AI accelerators.
Enterprises use quantum for specific workload types — not as a general replacement.

### B. AI + Quantum Convergence

Quantum-enhanced optimization could improve AI training efficiency or inference quality
for specific problem classes. Still theoretical but worth monitoring.

### C. Cryptography Disruption (Two-sided)

- **Threat:** Sufficiently powerful quantum computers break RSA/ECC encryption
- **Opportunity:** Post-quantum cryptography companies benefit from migration spending
- **Timeline:** Cryptographically relevant quantum is 10+ years away for most threat models

### D. Scientific Computing

Drug discovery and materials science represent near-term commercial use cases
that do not require error-corrected quantum — current NISQ (Noisy Intermediate Scale Quantum) hardware may suffice.

### E. Government and Defense Investment

Quantum is a strategic technology with significant government funding in US, EU, China.
Government contracts can provide non-dilutive revenue bridge to commercial scale.

## Technical Milestones to Monitor

| Milestone | Significance |
|---|---|
| Logical qubit demonstrations | Error correction progress — critical for commercial use |
| Gate fidelity improvements | Reduces errors per operation |
| Circuit depth improvements | Enables more complex computations |
| First enterprise paying customer (non-pilot) | Commercial signal |
| Revenue crossing $50M / year | Scale signal |
| Partnership with hyperscaler (AWS/Azure/GCP) | Distribution signal |
| Government contract awards | Funding bridge signal |

---

## Risk Signals (Exit or Reduce)

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Hardware progress stalls (no milestone for 12+ months) | Reduce |
| Revenue not converting despite technical progress | Reassess thesis timing |
| Dilutive capital raise without milestone justification | Reduce |
| Hyperscaler exits quantum investment | Bearish signal |
| Competing technology solves same use cases (e.g., classical AI) | Review thesis |

## Sizing Rules for Quantum

All quantum pure-plays are sized as Moonshots:
- Maximum 2% of portfolio per name
- Never average down
- Options preferred when available
- Exit on binary negative event (hardware setback, funding crisis)
- Total quantum exposure: ≤ 5% of portfolio

**Exception:** IONQ may be sized as an Accelerator (up to 4% maximum) if it demonstrates
consecutive quarters of sustained enterprise revenue growth above $20M/quarter.

---

## Analyst Evaluation Template

For quantum companies:

1. Has the company achieved any technical milestones in the last 12 months?
2. Is revenue real, growing, and from enterprises (not just government grants)?
3. What is the cash runway? (Quantum is pre-revenue or early-revenue — dilution risk)
4. Which quantum modality (trapped ion, superconducting, photonic, annealing)?
5. What is the path from current hardware to commercially useful compute?
6. Is a hyperscaler partnering with or investing in them?
7. What would need to happen for this to graduate from Moonshot to Accelerator?
